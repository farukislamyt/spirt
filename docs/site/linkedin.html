LinkedIn Profile OSINT Provider

SPIRT routes supported LinkedIn profile URLs through its public-web metadata provider and preserves provenance for normalized values.

Use cases

  • Public profile research
  • Structured metadata extraction
  • Evidence-backed reporting
  • Cross-platform signal comparison

Boundary

Only legitimately public information is in scope. Authentication, privacy, anti-bot, access-control, and rate-limit bypasses are outside SPIRT.
